<h1 class="visually-hidden">Mirakuru Europa OÜ</h1>

Mirakuru Europa OÜ is an Estonian company in Tallinn founded in Sept 2019. We build and
operate our own digital products, and we help other teams do the same.
{:.lead}

## Consulting

We provide software and product-development consulting. Drawing on our 20 over years experience within the IT,
e-commerce and telecoms industry, we support client teams across the whole journey, from early product design
through engineering and launch.

### Our expertise

We are a small, senior team, and we have built, shipped and operated the kind of products we help clients
with. Our work concentrates on a focused set of areas:

- **SaaS &amp; platform engineering** — Two decades designing, shipping and operating SaaS products, from
  cloud infrastructure and DevOps through to scalable, maintainable system architecture.
- **Python &amp; Django** — Our core stack. Deep, long-standing expertise in Python, the Django framework
  and the Wagtail CMS.
- **APIs &amp; telephony** — We founded and ran Xoxzo, a telephony SaaS platform (acquired in 2021), and
  hold patents in authentication and telephony. We know how to design, integrate and operate the APIs that
  products depend on — and today we run [PuchiDen](https://puchiden.app), browser-based international calling.
- **Email systems** — Hands-on experience running email at scale, from marketing and deliverability through
  to email-forwarding infrastructure.
- **AI** — Building AI-powered products and content pipelines, including LLM training workflows and
  generation platforms.
- **Content &amp; SEO** — Evidence-based, data-driven content strategy, competitive analysis and SEO that
  connects what you publish to measurable business results.

## Products

### PuchiDen

[PuchiDen](https://puchiden.app) lets you make international calls straight from your
browser — and save up to 95% compared with standard carrier rates. Call banks,
government offices, airlines, and customer-service lines across 180+ countries with
no app to install, no SIM, and no contract. Sign up with just an email and pay per
minute.
